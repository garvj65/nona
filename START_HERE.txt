NONA 0.6.1 PREVIEW
Your phone is the controller. Your PC runs the game.

There are two ways to connect. Pick one.

BLUETOOTH (no PC install)
  1. On GitHub: Actions > Android preview > latest green run > Artifacts >
     download nona-android-preview. Install the APK on your Android phone.
  2. In NONA: Set up Bluetooth > Pair a new computer.
  3. On the PC: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device > Bluetooth >
     pick your phone and confirm the PIN.
  4. Open controller > click your game on the PC > tap Play.
  Full guide: docs/ANDROID_BLUETOOTH.md

WI-FI (browser on the phone, small app on the PC)
  1. Install Python 3.10+ from https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
     and tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
  2. Double-click START_WINDOWS.bat and keep its window open.
  3. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi, scan the dashboard QR code and open it in Chrome.
     Turn the phone sideways.
  4. Try NONA Sprint from the dashboard straight away (leave output off for it).
     For other games: pick a profile, click Enable output, then click the
     game window within 4 seconds.

STOP
  Tap Stop on the phone, or "Stop · release all" on the laptop, before
  switching windows or ending play. It releases every key and button.
